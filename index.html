Meet the desk mat

Less desk clutter. More thoughtful design.

Designed for staffrooms, offices, libraries, colleges, hospitals and shared workspaces, the Eco-Charge Desk Mat combines useful desk essentials in one place.

01 / POWER

Solar USB charging

A solar panel converts sunlight into electrical energy and supplies USB power to compatible devices.

02 / ORGANISE

Stationery storage

A built-in storage box keeps everyday stationery together and within easy reach.

03 / DISPLAY

Phone stand

A handy stand keeps your phone upright on the desk while you work.

04 / PLAN

Clipboard space

A practical writing surface makes it easier to keep notes, lists and papers organised.

Simple by design

How does it work?

The idea is straightforward: make use of available sunlight and bring useful workspace features into one compact product.

STEP 01

Capture sunlight

The solar panel receives sunlight and converts it into electrical energy.

STEP 02

Connect through USB

The panel's output is made available through the USB cable connection.

STEP 03

Power a compatible device

Connect a compatible USB device in suitable sunlight. Charging performance depends on the available light and device.
